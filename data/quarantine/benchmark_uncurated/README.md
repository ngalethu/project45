# Uncurated video quarantine

These real videos are excluded from the 20-video benchmark. They are retained for recovery and are not loaded by `benchmark_manifest.json`.

Reasons include unknown provenance, redundant subjects/cameras, no visible driver, ambiguous driving state, or an unbelted-to-belted transition that is unsuitable as a whole-video normal label. The source `pexels_seatbelt_positive_5391318.mp4` was preserved here; only its audited 8–20 second belted segment is used by the benchmark.

Synthetic/cartoon demo videos were deleted separately and are not present here.
